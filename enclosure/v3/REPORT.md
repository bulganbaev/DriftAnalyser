## Power terminals: Wurth 7461059 REDCUBE (2026-10-18)
Stack, bottom to top, same at J2 and J5:
1. REDCUBE block 13.0 sq x 13.5 (board top T to T+13.5), M6 thread from the top, drawn bore 12.5 deep (read off the section on the drawing, runs ~full height; not a stated number).
2. Brass riser, 12 OD / 6.4 ID, 6.8 high (T+13.5 to T+20.3). Carries the current from the block face to the lug.
3. Ring lug 1.6, washer 1.2, M6 x 20 bolt (head 4 high). Thread engagement 10.4 mm (1.5 d = 9), tip stays 2.1 above the thread bottom. Tightening torque limit is the Wurth 3.9 N*m, below normal M6 torque, so use A2-70 or 8.8 and a torque wrench; add a spring/Nord-Lock washer.
4. PA66/PEEK insulating cup + sleeve in the lid: wall 22 OD / 19.6 ID round the block, roof 1.1 over the block top (0.2 gap), tube 16 OD / 12.4 ID through the 4 mm lid, flange 22 on the boss, two anti-rotation fingers either side of the tongue (unchanged), red / black boot on the flange.
Lid: 22.2 pocket from the underside up to T+14.8 (block pokes 1.0 above the T+12.5 seam). Radial gap block corner to cup 0.6, to lid wall 1.9. The lid is unchanged on top, so all heights, boots, and the hood are as before.

Why not the lug directly on the block top: the block top is T+13.5, inside the lid (T+12.5..T+16.5); the cable would have to leave through a notch in the lid, across the seam O-ring line and over the base wall. A short stud instead of a riser puts the current through the M6 thread, so the riser it is.
Gaps (measured on the solids, unchanged): centres 31.9, ring lug to ring lug 17.9, bolt head to bolt head 21.2, boot to boot 7.9. Lug to lid 1.8, riser to lid 1.75 (through the sleeve). J5 boot sticks out 9.8 past the rear wall (boot to y+30 vs wall at 116.5), J2 flush.
Clash check (`--clash`): clean, only overlaps are screws in tap holes and bolts in the block threads.

Bare board with all mating parts: `out/render-board-mated-iso-j1.png`, `-iso-studs.png`, `-top.png` (no base or lid; plug and loom on J1, lugs sit directly on the blocks with M6 x 12, red/black cables, SMA + whip at the wall position, USB-C plug in J3, shunts on JP1/JP2, 4-way Dupont on J4). Generated by `--mated` or any normal run; `--clash` checks it too (shunt/Dupont overlap the header pins on purpose). Parts: plug overmold and cable sizes are guesses.

## Changes from the first pass
- Board 55 × 112, holes from `placement.HOLES`: (3.8, 27.4), (51.2, 27.4), (3.5, 108.5), (51.5, 108.5).
- Second M6 stud J5 (battery negative, GND) at (44.4, 96.3), same stack as J2 at (14, 86.5): brass spacer through a PA66/PEEK sleeve, ring lug, washer, M6 bolt, boot. J2 boot and cable red, J5 black.
- Both lug tongues point to the rear (+board y). Two fingers on each sleeve flange (|x| 4.3–5.5, 5.8–8.6 behind the stud axis) flank the tongue so a lug can't swing toward the other stud; they're on the insulating sleeve, not the aluminium lid, since the lug is live. The boot has a matching tongue channel.
- Stud spacing: centres 31.9, ring lug to ring lug 17.9, bolt head to bolt head 21.2, boot to boot 7.9 (measured on the solids).
- USB-C J3 at x 14 on the bottom edge (shell 9.5–18.5, front at y 111.3). **No rear-wall opening** (USB_OPENING = False, 2026-10-18): USB is only for the first flash with the lid off, updates go over the air. Set USB_OPENING = True to cut the 13 × 7.5 slot again.
- U1 at (45.1, 57.3); u.FL at (51.1, 50.2) from the footprint's fab circle. The SMA bulkhead hole moved to y 50.2, still 7.5 above the board top. Inner nut (AF8, flats up): 0.3 above the U1 can's corner (overlaps it by 0.65 in x), 1.0 under the lid.
- Middle lid-screw pilasters moved to y 63 (clear of the SMA spot face), rear pair to y 110; ears to y 32 / 88.
- Heat pedestals from the routed copper, 2 mm high with a 1.0 mm insulating pad (drawn, light blue):

- Clash check (`--clash`, every pair of parts, volume > 0.001 mm³): clean; the only overlap is the lid screws in their tap holes. Plug/latch/J1 ramp, SMA/antenna threads, pigtail and the gasket (now drawn compressed) were trimmed so mating parts don't overlap.
