# DriftAnalyser PCB v3

Car LED controller: eight switched 12 V WS2812 strip channels, CAN, K-line, IMU, three inputs,
ESP32-S3. 55 × 112 mm, 6 layers, every part on the top side.

Everything is generated from `gen/` (`gen/model.py` = parts and nets, `gen/placement.py`,
`gen/pcb_builder.py` = copper, `gen/route.py` = replays the committed Freerouting session
`fab/route.ses`, `gen/export.py` = fab files). `bash gen/build.sh` rebuilds schematic, board, routing,
fab files and runs the tests. Do not hand-edit the `.kicad_sch` / `.kicad_pcb` files. What goes to
the fab and how to order it: [`fab/README-fab.md`](fab/README-fab.md).

Harness: crimp by pin number, not by position or wire colour. Each strip is three wires on three
fixed pins: its +12 V in row 1, its ground in row 4 straight below that, its data in row 2 or 3 (see
the table). Keep each strip's +12 V and ground in the same cable. CANH/CANL (18/19) are a twisted
pair. Pins 5 and 30 are signal grounds for the sensor / input side. Every cavity is used, so no
sealing plugs are needed.

## Quiescent current

Target ≤ 1 mA from the studs with IGN off (spec). With IGN off and HOLD low the buck is shut down
(R7 holds EN at 0 V, 5.3 µA typ) and everything on 5V / 3V3 is unpowered. What stays on VBUS / VIN,
from the datasheets (estimate, **not measured**):

| what | current |
| --- | --- |
| R1/R2 battery sense divider (470k + 100k) | ~22 µA at 12.6 V (≤ 30 µA even if D2 back-feeds the unpowered 3V3) |
| U2 LM74700 (EN tied to the anode, always on) | ~80 µA typ |
| U7 LMR36520 in shutdown | ~5 µA |
| U10..U13 BTS7008 standby, U5 TJA1027 VBAT, TVS leakage | a few µA each |
| total | ~0.1..0.2 mA |

## Firmware notes (fw-v3)

- Pin map: `gen/pinmap.py` (ESP32 GPIOs and the PCA9555 ports). U14 PCA9555 is at **I2C 0x20**:
  P00..P07 = LED1..8 IN, P10..P13 = DEN1..4, P14 = DSEL, P15 = CAN_S, P16 = status LED, P17 = sensor 5 V EN.
  Every switch input has a 10k pull-down, so all channels are off until the expander is written.
- BTS7008: switch k (U(9+k)) drives LED(2k−1) on OUT0 and LED(2k) on OUT1. Current sense: raise one
  DENk, then **DSEL = 0 → IS = OUT0 = LED(2k−1), DSEL = 1 → IS = OUT1 = LED(2k)**. The four IS pins
  share LED_ISENSE (GPIO2): kILIS 5450 into 1.2k → ~0.22 V per A (5 A ≈ 1.1 V); a fault reads full
  scale (clamped). Only one DEN high at a time.
- Power: IGN wakes the buck. Firmware sets **HOLD (GPIO41) high** to stay on after IGN drops, and
  low to power off. **Keep HOLD low on USB-only power** (VBAT_SENSE ≈ 0): the buck's EN must not sit
  above VIN + 0.3 V. IGN_DET (GPIO40) is a digital IGN-present input. VBAT_SENSE (GPIO1) = VBUS × 100/570 (470k / 100k, 82k source: sample
  slowly, C5 100 nF holds it).
- CAN: **CAN_S (P15) high = silent** (listen-only), and R70 pulls it high, so the board never drives
  the bus until firmware sets CAN_S low.
- Inputs IN1..3: the 10k pull on `INn_PULL` acts on the ADC node (`INn_SENSE`), behind the 10k / 33k
  divider. With the pull-up on (GPIO high), an open input reads **≈ 1.65 V** and an input shorted to
  GND **≈ 1.48 V**; with the pull-down on (GPIO low), 12 V on the input reads **≈ 1.25 V**. GPIO as
  input = no pull.
- 5V_SENS: enable with P17, overcurrent = SENS_5V_FLT (GPIO47) low.
- **Strip data while a channel is off:** drive LEDn_DATA low (GPIO low, or idle the RMT channel low)
  whenever that channel's switch is off. U15/U16 (74HCT245) run from 5 V and keep driving their
  outputs: a high DATA line into an unpowered strip back-powers its first LED through the data pin.

## Bring-up checklist (first board)

1. **Before fab, still open:**
   - **J1 (TE 6437288-1) geometry checked 2026-10-18** against TE customer drawing
     C-9-1437287-8 rev A1, sheet 2 (34 pos): mounting holes Ø3.3 at 38.5, nearest pin row 6.5 from
     them, rows 2.5 / 3 / 2.5 apart, 3 mm pitch, 8-pin rows offset 1.5, outer 9-pin row starts 7.25
     from the mounting hole, through holes Ø1.3 +0.2 (ours drill 1.4) — all match
     `da3:TE_6437288-1`. **Still must-check before crimping a harness: pin numbering.** That drawing
     gives no cavity numbers; our footprint puts 1–9 in the row farthest from the mounting holes
     (top row of the mating face), pin 1 at the left seen from the component side. Confirm with the
     mating plug 4-1437290-0 drawing or the moulded numbers on the housing.
   - LMR36520 exposed-pad mask: KiCad's footprint opens 2.4 × 3.1 mm, TI's DDA0008B example
     2.71 × 3.4 mm. Decide whether to enlarge it.
   - Thermal pad in the case must be electrically insulating: the B.Cu under U10..U13 is VBUS.
   - Order with **ENIG** and the press-fit hole note (`fab/README-fab.md`): 32 holes at J2/J5,
     finished **1.475 ± 0.05 mm**.
   - Done in the final review: J2/J5 footprint `da3:Wurth_REDCUBE_7461059` drawn from Würth drawing
     7461059 rev 001.003 (2025-02-04); Q1/Q2 land `da3:Infineon_TDSON-8_FL` checked against
     the BSC016N06NS datasheet rev 2.6 Fig 2 (KiCad's `TDSON-8-1` lead pads were 0.85 long with a
     1.25 gap; now 1.10 long with Infineon's 1.00 gap, gate 0.90 - see the footprint's descr).
2. **Press the studs in** (J2, J5, Würth 7461059) after JLC's assembly: arbor press, one straight
   stroke, ~640 N (40 N per pin), flat stamp on the block, the board supported right under the pin
   field (Würth: support plate with 1.75 mm holes under the 16 pins), no soldering. If pressing is
   not possible, Würth's REDCUBE THR with internal thread (series WP-THRBU, soldered) is the
   alternative - different pins, so a new footprint; no part number checked.
3. **Stud polarity:** J2 = +12 V (silk "+12V"), J5 = GND (silk "GND −"). Check with a meter that J5
   is GND (to J1 pin 5) and J2 is not shorted to it.
4. **Quiescent current:** bench supply 12.6 V on the studs, IGN open: ≤ 1 mA (expect ~0.1..0.2 mA).
   Measure it twice: jumpers open, and then **with JP1 fitted and the car's K-line connected** -
   JP1's 510 R pull-up sits on always-on VBUS, so a K-line held low by the car draws ~24 mA through
   it in sleep. If it does, leave JP1 open (most ECUs pull K-line up themselves).
5. Rails: IGN to 12 V → 5 V across C11, 3V3 across C13 (no test points on this board).
6. Flash over USB, check U14 at 0x20 and U3 at 0x6A on I2C.
7. **Each channel's IS:** load each strip channel in turn (a power resistor), DENk + DSEL, compare
   the ADC reading with ~0.22 V/A; check that every other channel stays off.
8. **CAN silent by default:** on a bus, the board must not ACK anything until firmware drives CAN_S low.
9. K-line and the inputs with the jumpers as fitted for the car.

## Logic buck: LMR36520 instead of AP63200

The SMCJ24CA input TVS clamps at 38.9 V at peak pulse current. That is above the AP63200's VIN abs
max (~35 V) on VBUS. U7 is now the TI **LMR36520ADDAR** (C2879422, 7,712 in stock, $1.20). It is a
synchronous buck: VIN 4.2-65 V, abs max 70 V, 2 A, 400 kHz, PFM at light load, internal compensation,
HSOIC-8 (datasheet SNVSBF0C, Nov 2025). External parts come from Fig 8-1 / Table 8-1 (5 V row):

- 10 µH (Bourns SRN6045TA-100M, Isat 4.6 A ≥ ISC 3.6 A max)
- 2 × 22 µF out
- FB divider 100k / 22.6k → 5.42 V (VREF 1 V)
- 10 µF 50 V + 220 nF 100 V in
- 100 nF BOOT cap
- 1 µF VCC cap
- PG left open

The symbol `da3:LMR36520ADDA` is a copy of KiCad's LMR36510ADDA (the datasheet says the two are
pin-to-pin compatible). It is checked against Table 5-1 by `test_lmr36520_pinout_per_datasheet`.
Shutdown current is 5.3 µA typ at EN = 0 V, and EN has no internal pull-up (2.7 nA leakage).
R7 holds EN at 0 V, so the buck adds about 5 µA to the stud current in sleep.

The EN pin must not exceed VIN + 0.3 V (Recommended Operating Conditions, note 2). On USB-only power
HOLD can be at 3.3 V while VBUS is ~0 V. R19 (100k, PWR_EN → EN) limits what EN can push into VIN
to ~25 µA. R8 (HOLD pull-down) is 10k, so a weak pull-up on GPIO41 during reset cannot lift HOLD
(and through D5 the buck's EN) anywhere near the 1.157 V threshold. Firmware should still keep HOLD low when VBAT_SENSE shows no battery.

Footprint is KiCad `Texas_HTSOP-8-1EP_..._EP2.95x4.9mm_Mask2.4x3.1mm_ThermalVias`, the default of
KiCad's LMR3x DDA symbols. TI's DDA0008B example layout has a 2.71 × 3.4 mm mask opening on the EP.
KiCad's is 2.4 × 3.1 mm, so slightly less pad is soldered. Fine for this load, but look at it during layout.

## Copper and routing (Task 11)

Six layers (task 11 ruling): JLC 6-layer 1.6 mm, 1 oz outer, **1 oz inner** (order option); no
impedance control needed. The board file declares JLC06161H-3313 for the record, but a named JLC
stack may not apply with 1 oz inner, and nothing here depends on the dielectric stack. Top to bottom: F.Cu power pours near the parts + signals, **In1.Cu solid GND** (0.1 mm under
F), In2.Cu signals + GND fill, In3.Cu VBUS (middle of the board from the switches down to the drains,
and under the CAN / K-line block) / VIN (under the stud) / the outer four LED channels' lanes / GND in
the rest, In4.Cu signals + GND fill, B.Cu signals + VBUS copper under each switch's exposed pad.
Everything is drawn by `gen/pcb_builder.py`.

- **Thermal pad: the B.Cu copper under U10..U13 is VBUS** (12 vias from each switch's exposed pad).
  The case's thermal pad that presses on it must be electrically insulating (silicone gap pad,
  not graphite or metal).
- J1 pinout (task 11 ruling, replaces the spec's 12V | DO | GND triplets): row 1 = the eight
  LEDn_12V, each above its own switch output, row 4 = the eight strip grounds in the same columns,
  rows 2-3 = data, CAN, K-line, IGN, IN1..3, 5V_SENS. Each LED channel is one short F.Cu pour.
- **Studs (Würth 7461059):** 16 press-fit pins (perimeter of a 5 × 5 grid, 2.54 mm pitch), 2.0 mm
  pads, 1.475 mm finished holes (ENIG), all pad 1. The 16 barrels tie every layer. No via sits under
  the 13 × 13 mm block (`pcb_builder.STUD_BODY`, `test_studs_are_redcube_with_nothing_under_the_body`).
  J2's pins are in the F and In3 VIN pours; on the other four layers a 14.6 mm VIN square keeps the
  GND fill out of the pin field. The committed session predates this footprint: the signals never
  came near the studs, so `route.restud` redraws the stud copper after the replay (drops the old
  VIN / GND vias under the block, draws J5's new via ring) instead of a new route.
- **J5 (battery negative):** a GND disc (r 9.9 mm, the block's corners are at r 9.19) on all six
  layers, a square ring of 0.8/0.4 vias 0.5 mm outside the block (32), and 29 more 0.8/0.4 GND vias
  centred on the disc's edge (`route.j5_seam_vias`, drawn after the session import). The disc outranks the board GND fill, so
  the fill stops at the disc's edge; the seam vias sit half in each, on every layer, so the stud
  cannot end up on a copper island (`test_j5_disc_joined_to_gnd_fill`). From there In1 and the GND
  fills carry the return to J1 row 4 (about 1 mΩ at 1 oz inner copper).
- U1 (ESP32) sits under U14 and the data resistor rows; the CAN / K-line / input block moved
  below it as one piece (`placement.COMMS_BLOCK`).
- C8 (bootstrap cap) is turned 180 degrees against the first placement so its BST pad faces U7 pin 7.
- Routing: the session `fab/route.ses` is committed and the build replays it, so `build.sh` is
  deterministic. New draws run on the compute server: `bash gen/route_remote.sh push|status|pull|stop`
  (variants in `gen/route_variants.sh`), then copy the chosen `.ses` to `fab/route.ses`.
